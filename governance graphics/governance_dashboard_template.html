How to read this

Each point is the percentage of valid Yes/No filings reporting Yes in that year. Hover over points for counts and denominators.

Why it is useful

Compares two different reported organizational arrangements. Joint ventures here refer specifically to the type addressed by Form 990, Part VI, Line 16a.

The number of valid responses can differ between measures and years. Neither response is a direct measure of consolidation.

Board independence by management delegation

Delegation = YesDelegation = No
How to read this

Each point averages independent members ÷ voting members among filings with a valid board count and the indicated delegation response.

Why it is useful

Connects two Form 990 governance measures within the same reporting groups. Differences are descriptive and may reflect differences in the organizations included each year.

Board independence is not a direct measure of oversight quality. No causal relationship is implied.
